## More Than Two Decades Fishing the Florida Keys

For Capt. Daniel Zier, finding fish isn't something learned from a chart or an app.

It comes from years on the water.

Capt. Dan brings approximately 24 years of fishing experience to Key West Fishing Connection and specializes in putting anglers on the powerful fish that make the Florida Keys famous.

Sharks. Tarpon. Goliath grouper. Snapper.

Some guests arrive as experienced fishermen chasing a particular trophy. Others step aboard having never held a fishing rod.
Capt. Dan enjoys fishing with both.

His goal is straightforward: figure out what kind of experience you want, find the fish and make sure everyone aboard gets involved.

## A Different Kind of Key West Charter

Capt. Dan's fishing style focuses heavily on action.

Instead of making long offshore runs, many trips concentrate on productive waters around Key West and Stock Island.
Chumming, live baiting, bottom fishing, spinning, light tackle and heavy tackle are all used depending on the target.
When sharks arrive behind the boat or a Goliath grouper eats a bait near structure, things can get serious quickly.

That's part of the fun.

## Great Wwith First-Time Anglers

Never caught a shark?

Never been fishing?

Bring the kids.

Capt. Dan will explain the equipment, show you what to do and help throughout the fight.

The goal isn't simply to put experienced fishermen on fish. It's to give everyone aboard the opportunity to experience what Key West fishing is all about.
