faq_items:
  - question: "Do I need a Florida fishing license?"
    answer: "No. The necessary fishing licenses are included with your charter."

  - question: "Do you provide fishing equipment?"
    answer: "Yes. Rods, reels, terminal tackle and the fishing equipment needed for your trip are provided."

  - question: "Can we keep fish?"
    answer: "Legal species may be kept when seasons, size limits and other regulations allow. Capt. Dan can clean and fillet your legal catch."

  - question: "Are your trips good for kids?"
    answer: "Yes. Family and children's fishing trips are an important part of Key West Fishing Connection. Trips can be tailored to the age and experience of your group."

  - question: "I've never fished before. Is that a problem?"
    answer: "Not at all. Many guests are first-time anglers. Capt. Dan provides instruction and helps you throughout the trip."

  - question: "How many people can fish?"
    answer: "The boat accommodates up to four anglers on most trips. Certain specialized trips may have smaller limits."

  - question: "What should we bring?"
    answer: "Bring comfortable clothing, polarized sunglasses if you have them, sun protection, food or snacks and your preferred drinks."

  - question: "What happens if the weather is bad?"
    answer: "Conditions in the Keys can change quickly. Capt. Dan makes the final decision regarding safe fishing conditions and will communicate with you when weather could affect a trip."

  - question: "What fish can we catch?"
    answer: "Common targets include lemon, bull, blacktip and nurse sharks, Goliath grouper, tarpon, mangrove and mutton snapper, barracuda, cobia and jacks. Species availability changes throughout the year."

  - question: "Can we specifically target sharks?"
    answer: "Absolutely. Shark fishing is one of Capt. Dan's specialties and several trips are designed specifically around targeting large Key West sharks."
