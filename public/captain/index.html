A Key West Fishing Captain Who Knows Where the Big Fish Live

For Capt. Dan Zier, fishing the waters around Key West isn't just about putting another fish in the boat. It's about giving people the chance to experience the kind of fishing that makes the Florida Keys special—big fish, shallow water, screaming drags and stories they'll still be telling years later.

With more than two decades of fishing experience, Capt. Dan has built Key West Fishing Connection around a simple idea: Spend less time riding around and more time fishing.

Dan specializes in the powerful gamefish and predators that live in the waters surrounding Key West and the Lower Keys. Sharks, Goliath grouper, tarpon and snapper make up much of his fishing calendar, with trips designed for everyone from families catching their first fish to experienced anglers looking for a legitimate trophy.

And when Capt. Dan says "big fish," he means it.

More Than 20 Years on the Water

Capt. Dan has spent roughly 24 years developing the experience that goes into every Key West Fishing Connection charter.

Over that time, he's learned that successful fishing in the Florida Keys isn't simply a matter of driving to a waypoint and dropping a bait.

Everything matters.

The tide matters. The current matters. Wind direction matters. Water temperature matters. Bait movement matters. Even a small change in conditions can turn yesterday's best fishing spot into today's empty patch of water.

Experience teaches a captain when to stay, when to move and when to completely change the plan.

That's the knowledge Capt. Dan brings aboard every morning.

Rather than relying on a single fishing style, Dan adjusts each trip around the conditions and his guests. That might mean establishing a chum line for sharks, dropping heavy tackle around structure for Goliath grouper, hunting migrating tarpon or switching to lighter tackle to keep a family busy catching snapper.

The target changes.

The objective doesn't:

Find the fish.

A Passion for Big Fish

There's something different about hooking a fish capable of taking control of the situation.

That's the kind of fishing Capt. Dan loves.

A large lemon shark suddenly appearing in clear water behind the boat is difficult to forget. So is watching a bull shark take a bait and disappear while line pours from the reel.

Goliath grouper provide an entirely different challenge. Instead of making long runs, these enormous fish use brute strength to get back into structure. The first few seconds can determine the entire fight.

Then there's the tarpon.

The Silver King combines size, speed and spectacular jumps into one of Florida's greatest fishing experiences.

These are the fish that have helped define Capt. Dan's style of fishing.

They're also the reason many customers step aboard saying:

"I want to catch something big."

Dan knows exactly what they mean.

Shark Fishing with Capt. Dan

Sharks are one of Capt. Dan's specialties and one of the signature experiences at Key West Fishing Connection.

The warm waters surrounding Key West support an impressive variety of shark species.

Depending on the season and conditions, anglers can encounter lemon sharks, bull sharks, blacktips, nurse sharks, hammerheads and other species.

But shark fishing isn't simply about dropping a bait overboard and waiting.

Dan uses his knowledge of tides, channels, current and feeding areas to select locations where sharks are likely to travel. Fresh bait and chum help create a scent trail through the water.

Then the waiting begins.

Sometimes it doesn't take long.

A shadow appears.

A fin breaks the surface.

A bait starts moving.

And suddenly the reel is screaming.

For many guests, it's the first time they've ever seen a large shark outside an aquarium or television screen.

Seeing one beside a 22-foot boat is an entirely different experience.

The Goliath Grouper Challenge

If sharks are about powerful runs, Goliath grouper are about raw strength. These massive fish inhabit wrecks, rock piles, ledges and other structure around South Florida and the Florida Keys.

Getting one to eat can be only half the battle.

Once hooked, the angler has to stop the fish from getting back into its home.

That's why Capt. Dan uses serious tackle when targeting them.

A Goliath grouper trip can turn into a contest of strength involving the angler, the captain and a fish that seems determined to prove it has the advantage.

It's not delicate fishing.

That's exactly why people love it.

For anglers who arrive in Key West wanting to catch the biggest fish of their lives, Goliath grouper are often at the top of the list.

Family Fishing Is Just as Important

Not every great fishing story needs to involve a 300-pound fish.

Sometimes the most important fish of the day is a kid's first snapper.

Capt. Dan enjoys introducing new anglers to fishing and regularly welcomes families, children and people who have never held a fishing rod before.

There is no expectation that you already know what you're doing.

Dan can handle the knots, tackle, bait and strategy while explaining what anglers need to know along the way.

Family trips can concentrate on species such as mangrove snapper and other inshore fish that provide frequent action and manageable fights.

And in Key West, you never know what might show up.

A simple snapper trip can quickly become much more interesting when something considerably larger starts swimming around the boat.

You Don't Have to Be an Expert

One of the biggest misconceptions about booking a fishing charter is that you need to know how to fish.

You don't.

Capt. Dan fishes with experienced anglers, complete beginners and everyone in between.

If you've been fishing your entire life, Dan can concentrate on putting you in the right place and letting you do your thing.

If you've never touched a fishing rod, he'll show you how.

The objective isn't to impress people with complicated fishing terminology.

It's to help you catch fish.

A Personal Key West Fishing Experience

Key West Fishing Connection operates differently from a large offshore charter operation.

Capt. Dan fishes aboard a 22-foot Lake & Bay with a maximum of four anglers on most trips.

That keeps the experience personal.

You're not one of a dozen passengers standing along the rail waiting for a turn.

It's your boat for the trip.

Dan can tailor the day around your group, experience level and fishing goals.

If your priority is catching your first shark, tell him.

If you've caught plenty of sharks but have never fought a Goliath grouper, tell him.

If your eight-year-old just wants to catch fish, that's important too.

And if your request is simply:

"Dan, put me on the biggest thing we can catch today."

Well, that's a conversation he's happy to have.

Fishing Key West the Local Way

One of the advantages of fishing Key West is that an incredible amount of productive water exists relatively close to home.

You don't necessarily need to spend hours running offshore before the fishing begins. Channels, shallow flats, hard bottom, wrecks, rock piles and deeper water create habitat for an enormous variety of marine life around Key West and Stock Island.

That allows Capt. Dan to spend more of the charter doing what guests actually booked the trip to do: Fish.

It's particularly valuable on three- and four-hour trips, where maximizing fishing time can make a significant difference.

Conservation and Respect for the Fishery

The waters surrounding Key West are an extraordinary natural resource, and keeping them productive requires respecting the fish that live here.

Capt. Dan follows current fishing regulations and responsible handling practices, particularly when dealing with sharks, tarpon and Goliath grouper.

Some species are caught primarily for the experience, photographs and memories before being released.

Other legal species, including snapper when seasons and regulations allow, can provide one of the other great traditions of Keys fishing:

Fresh fish for dinner.

Capt. Dan can clean and fillet legal harvested fish at the end of the trip.

The Fish Are Only Part of the Story

A successful charter isn't measured only by pounds of fish.

It's the kid who catches his first fish.

The father and son who finally get to take the fishing trip they've talked about for years.

The couple who watches the Key West sunset while waiting for a shark bite.

The experienced angler who discovers that a Goliath grouper pulls considerably harder than expected.

And the person who comes aboard saying they've never been fishing before and leaves asking when they can do it again.

Those experiences are what have helped Capt. Dan earn hundreds of five-star reviews from anglers who have fished with Key West Fishing Connection.

Fish With Capt. Dan

If you're looking for a polished yacht ride where fishing is secondary, there are plenty of ways to spend an afternoon in Key West.

If you want to go fishing, Capt. Dan is ready.

Come chase sharks.

Try to stop a Goliath grouper.

Jump a tarpon.

Catch snapper with the kids.

Or step aboard and ask Capt. Dan the question he hears all the time:

"What's the biggest thing we can catch today?"

Then grab a rod and find out.
