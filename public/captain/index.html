CAPTAIN PROFILE

Meet Captain Mike

USCG 100-Ton Master Captain with over 20 years of charter fishing experience in Key West.

BORN & RAISED ON THE WATER

A Passion for the Catch

Captain Mike grew up fishing the shallow flats and deep offshore drops of the Florida Keys. Holding a USCG 100-Ton Master License, Mike has spent the last two decades guiding thousands of anglers to bucket-list catches, trophy fish, and unforgettable family memories.

Whether you are a seasoned angler chasing record Sailfish or a family introducing kids to saltwater fishing for the first time, Captain Mike tailors every trip to match your goals, skill level, and comfort.
